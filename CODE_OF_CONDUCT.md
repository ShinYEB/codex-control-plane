# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

RUVORA contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Protect private information, credentials, transcripts, and proprietary code.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community boundaries gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate misrepresentation
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Using project channels to pressure maintainers or bypass security and contribution policies

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where a person represents the RUVORA project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation, flag the content through GitHub and contact the maintainers in the relevant thread when safe. For a confidential report, use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and begin the report title with `[Conduct]`. Do not include unrelated secrets.

Maintainers will review reports privately, gather only the information needed, avoid conflicts of interest, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include clarification, a warning, content removal, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy to the extent reasonably possible.

Good-faith reports will not be retaliated against. Knowingly false or abusive reports may themselves violate this policy.
